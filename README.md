🌍 World Happiness Analysis: 2015 vs. 2022
Project Overview

This project analyzes global happiness data from the 2015 and 2022 World Happiness Reports to examine how happiness scores changed across countries and regions over an eight-year period.

The analysis evaluates overall happiness alongside six contributing factors: GDP per capita, family/social support, life expectancy, freedom, generosity, and trust/government corruption. The original datasets contained 158 ranked countries in 2015 and 146 countries in 2022.

Using Google Sheets, Excel, and Tableau, I cleaned and standardized the datasets, conducted comparative analysis, and developed visualizations to identify geographic and socioeconomic patterns in global happiness.

The goal was to determine whether happiness scores increased or decreased between 2015 and 2022 and explore how changes in the six contributing factors related to overall happiness.

Business Objective

Although the World Happiness Report focuses on social and economic well-being rather than a traditional business problem, the project applies data analytics techniques to a policy and decision-making scenario.

The analysis sought to answer:

How did global happiness scores change between 2015 and 2022?
Which countries and regions reported the highest happiness scores?
How do the six contributing factors vary across regions?
Which factors appear most closely associated with higher overall happiness?
What geographic patterns can be identified through comparative visualization?

The broader objective was to transform raw international survey data into insights that could support evidence-based discussions about economic development, social well-being, and public policy.

🧹 DATA PREPARATION: World Happiness Datasets

Business Scenario

The 2015 and 2022 World Happiness datasets were structured differently, making direct comparison difficult without first standardizing the data.

Project Overview

The datasets were cleaned and aligned to support comparative analysis. Headers in the 2022 dataset were reformatted to remain consistent with the 2015 dataset, and a Region field was added to the 2022 data to enable regional comparisons.

Analytical Value

Standardize data across reporting periods
Enable year-over-year comparison
Support regional aggregation
Improve consistency across visualizations
Prepare the data for analysis in Tableau

📈 COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS: 2015 vs. 2022

Business Scenario

The primary analytical question was whether global happiness improved or declined during the eight-year period represented by the datasets.

Project Overview

The analysis compared overall happiness and its underlying factors between 2015 and 2022.

The original analysis found that overall happiness scores declined while the individual factors did not move uniformly. In particular, the analysis identified increases in some economic and social measures alongside decreases in measures such as trust in government, freedom, and generosity.

Analytical Value

Measure change across reporting periods
Distinguish overall outcomes from individual contributing factors
Identify potentially important socioeconomic trends
Demonstrate the importance of analyzing multiple variables together

🔎 Key Findings

The analysis identified several patterns within the World Happiness data:

Australia and New Zealand, North America, and Western Europe demonstrated comparatively high happiness performance.
GDP/economic conditions, social support, life expectancy, and freedom were associated with the perception of overall happiness in the original analysis.
Overall happiness declined between the two reporting periods examined.
Changes in individual happiness factors were not uniform; improvement in some measures did not necessarily correspond with improvement in overall happiness.
The findings suggested that happiness should be evaluated as a multidimensional outcome rather than through a single economic indicator.
